My favorite city.

My favorite city is Gaborone, Botswana. It is my place of birth and the city I know the most. It is the capital of my home country and where most people I know live. I went to school here, worked here and dated here. It has a dam on its outskirts on its south side (Gaborone Dam)and a locally famous suburb (Phakalane) to its north. We use combi's as the primary public transport method. Its best malls are Main Mall, Airport Junction, Riverwalk and Game City. My favorite thing to do in town is go to the movies at the New Capitol Cinemas.
